Public or Private — You Decide

Every dedication is different, and so is every reason for creating one. That's why you choose exactly who gets to see it.

🔒 Private Dedications

Keep your message just between you and the person it's meant for. Share it only through your unique link or code — nobody else can find it or browse it.

🌍 Public Dedications

Want your dedication to inspire others too? Make it public and it will appear on our community dedications page, where visitors can browse heartfelt messages from people around the world.

Your Privacy Comes First

Love Dedicatory was designed so you always stay in control of what you share and who can see it.

Every dedication you create on Love Dedicatory gets a unique, private code. Nobody can guess or browse to it unless you choose to share it or make it public.

  • Private dedications are never listed or indexed anywhere on the site.
  • You decide if and when a dedication becomes public.
  • Photos and messages are stored securely and only shown through your unique link or code.

Want to learn more about how we handle your data? Read our Privacy Policy.
