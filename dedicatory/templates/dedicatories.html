Michael To his son, Noah
👨‍👦 Father & Son

"The day you were born, I became a better man."

Noah, I still remember the exact moment I held you for the first time. You were so small, and yet the weight of that moment was everything. In that instant, my whole life rearranged itself around you. Watching you grow has been the greatest privilege of my life. Every question you ask, every challenge you face head-on, every time you get back up — you teach me something new about courage. Never forget: no matter where life takes you, you will always have a father who believes in you completely. I'm proud of you every single day, son.

— With all my love, Dad 💙

Sarah To her son, Ethan
💜 Mother & Son

"You are the best thing I have ever done."

My dearest Ethan, There are no words big enough to describe the love a mother carries for her child. But I'll try anyway — because you deserve to know. From your very first breath, you filled every corner of my world with a light I didn't know was missing. I have watched you stumble and rise, cry and laugh, dream and doubt — and through every single moment, my love for you has only grown deeper. You don't have to be perfect, baby. You just have to be you. And who you are is already more than enough — more than I could have ever wished for. I love you yesterday, today, and every tomorrow that comes.

— Always your mom, Sarah 💜
